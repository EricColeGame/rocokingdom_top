## How Rarity Tiers Usually Work — and Where Legendaries Fit

Creature-collectors rarely invent new rarity systems. Most use a four-to-five-tier ladder, and the marketing language around Roco Kingdom ("400+ unique Jinies," "strategy over stats") points toward the standard model. The table below is a planning framework based on genre conventions, not an official chart.

| Expected tier | Typical availability | How you'll probably get one | Prep you can do now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | Everywhere in the open world | Standard capture during exploration | Stock up on basic capture items |
| Uncommon | Regional or weather-dependent | Targeted hunting in specific biomes | Learn zone layouts early |
| Rare | Limited spawn windows | Timed hunts, repeat attempts | Build a reliable travel route |
| Legendary | One-off or heavily gated | Story milestones, special encounters, events | Save resources and don't waste attempts |
| Mythic / event | Time-limited | Live events and promotions | Follow official channels closely |

The practical takeaway: legendary Jinies are almost always gated by *opportunity*, not by player level. That means preparation beats grinding. Players who arrive at a rare encounter with full resources and a team that can survive a long fight will convert far more attempts than players who arrive empty.

## How to Find and Catch Legendary Jinies: A Field Plan

Catching a rare creature in any open-world collector comes down to four phases. Here's how to structure them for Roco Kingdom legendary Jinies once the servers open.

| Phase | What to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Scout | Map the region, note unusual spawn points, and track time-of-day changes | Legendary encounters are usually location- and timing-gated |
| 2. Weaken | Bring a team that can chip damage without one-shotting the target | Overkill ends the encounter and wastes the attempt |
| 3. Capture | Use the strongest capture item you can afford on the first attempt | Early attempts on a rare spawn have the highest failure cost |
| 4. Verify | Log what worked — time, weather, location, team composition | Repeatable notes beat memory when a spawn is rare |

Two habits separate successful hunters from frustrated ones. First, **never burn your best capture items on a common creature** — bank them. Second, **keep one team slot flexible** so you can swap in a creature that counters the target's element on short notice.

The official site also emphasizes "strategy over stats" in PvP, which strongly implies that a legendary Jini won't automatically win fights. A well-built team of uncommon Jinies should be able to answer a rare one. Plan for counters, not just collection.

## Building a Team Around Legendary Jinies

The temptation with a rare creature is to build the whole team around it. Resist that. The more useful approach is to decide what *role* your legendary Jini fills, then build the rest of the team to cover its weaknesses.

| Team role | What it contributes | What you need around it |
|---|---|---|
| Damage dealer | Finishes fights quickly | A defensive partner and a speed option |
| Bulky anchor | Survives long exchanges | Reliable damage from the back line |
| Speed lead | Sets the pace of a match | Something that punishes slow opponents |
| Support / utility | Enables the rest of the team | At least one self-sufficient attacker |

Community reports from creature-collector launches consistently show the same thing: teams built around a single powerful creature lose to balanced teams once players learn the counters. Treat your first legendary as a piece, not a plan.
