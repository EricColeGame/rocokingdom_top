## What the Roco Kingdom Monster List Actually Contains

The most widely used English reference is the community-maintained [Roco Kingdom World Rocodex database](https://rocokingdomworld.org/pokedex/), which indexes every known spirit with type, stats, form, skills, and evolution data. That sounds like a wall of numbers until you notice that each entry follows the same five-column logic. Learn the columns once and the list stops being a wiki rabbit hole and starts being a lookup table.

| Column | What it tells you | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Entry number (NO###) | Groups an evolution line and all of its alternate forms under one number | Scan numbers, not names, to find relatives |
| Spirit name | English name plus the original name in parentheses | Cross-check when a community guide uses a different translation |
| Elemental type | One of 18 types; a handful of late entries are dual-type | Confirm the type before you invest in a catch |
| Form | Original, seasonal, regional, cosmetic, or story variant | Never count a form as a separate species |
| Stats, skills, evolution | Base stat total, move data, and the next stage | Compare before spending upgrade materials |

One quirk worth knowing: the numbering is not strictly sequential. The main sequence runs to NO466, then jumps to NO852, NO888, NO928, NO961, NO1008, NO1012, NO1015, and NO1024. Those high-numbered entries behave like special, event, or late-added spirits, which is why a plain alphabetical search sometimes misses them.

## Forms, Variants, and Why the Count Looks Bigger Than It Is

Here is the honest caveat about any Roco Kingdom monster list: 644 entries does not mean 644 unique species. A meaningful slice of the database is alternate forms — seasonal plumage, molting shells, gem cuts, and even joke costumes. Community reports suggest new players routinely assume these are separate catches and waste hours hunting something that is actually the same spirit in a different outfit.

| Form family | Example entries | What actually changes |
|---|---|---|
| Seasonal birds | Flurri, Winterfinch, Swiftwing, Snowcrown | Spring, summer, autumn, and original looks each get their own entry |

| Gem snails | Six Gemlord gem variants, then Gemperor | Appearance tied to the gem material shown |

| Desert rodents | Burroo, Burrup, Burrazer (dry season vs. water-storing) | Environment-driven form swap |

Three rules keep this manageable:

- **Count numbers, not entries.** If two spirits share a NO number, treat them as one species with skins.
- **Read the form label.** Words like "Form," "Flavor," or a season in parentheses mean variant, not evolution.
- **Check branch evolutions.** The chess family shows one number splitting into several final forms, so pick your branch before you grind.

## FAQ

**How many spirits are in the Roco Kingdom monster list?**
The Rocodex currently indexes 644 spirits with type, stats, form, skills, and evolution data. That figure counts alternate forms, so the number of distinct evolution lines is noticeably smaller.

**Are all of these monsters actually obtainable?**
Not necessarily. The gaps in the numbering — jumping from NO466 to NO852 and beyond — suggest that some entries are event, story, or special-release spirits. Treat high-numbered entries as "check availability first."

**What is the difference between a form and an evolution?**
An evolution moves a spirit to a new number and usually changes its stats and skills. A form keeps the same number and changes only appearance or flavor — Duckiji's costume variants and the seasonal birds are the clearest examples.

**Where can I find an up-to-date Roco Kingdom monster list?**
The [Rocodex spirit index](https://rocokingdomworld.org/pokedex/) is the most complete English reference, and it pairs the list with an egg identifier, tier list, type chart, and stat calculator so you can plan a team instead of guessing.
