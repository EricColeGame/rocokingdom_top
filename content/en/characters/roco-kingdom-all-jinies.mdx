## Types, Matchups, and Why They Decide Fights

Eighteen types means a lot of overlap, and overlap is where fights are won. A creature with a strong stat total can still lose to a lower-tier opponent if the matchup is wrong. Before any major battle, run through this short checklist:

- Identify the opponent's type from its record.
- Check your lead creature's matchup against that type.
- Note whether you have a safe switch if the lead goes down.
- Confirm your backup has a skill that hits the opponent's weakness.

## Reading the Numbers: Totals, IVs, and Spirit Scores

Stat totals are only the surface. Once you start caring about performance, three separate measurements matter, and mixing them up is one of the most common mistakes in the community.

| Metric | What it measures | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Base stat total | The creature's raw ceiling before training | Shows overall potential at a glance |
| IV quality | The individual roll on a specific creature | Explains why two of the same Jinie perform differently |
| Nature value | A modifier that shifts stat emphasis | Determines whether a build leans offensive or defensive |
| Training experience | Progress you have invested | Separates a fresh catch from a finished project |

A creature with a great base total and a poor individual roll can underperform a mid-tier creature with excellent rolls. That is why serious collectors check the individual values before pouring resources into star-up materials. ## A Practical Workflow for Collecting Every Jinie

Chasing a full Roco Kingdom all Jinies collection without a system turns into random wandering. A repeatable loop works better:

1. **Pick a target group.** Filter the index by type, form, or name rather than scrolling the full list.
2. **Read the obtain notes.** Confirm where the creature appears before you travel.
3. **Check the map.** Use an interactive map to plan a route that hits several spawn points in one trip.
4. **Verify eggs.** If the creature comes from an egg, match height and weight against the egg predictor instead of guessing.
5. **Log what you caught.** Track individual rolls so you do not re-hunt a creature you already own at a good quality.
6. **Check the event calendar.** Limited-time appearances are the easiest spawns to miss and the hardest to replace.

Step four deserves emphasis. Egg hatching is where community reports show the most wasted effort — players hatch dozens of eggs hoping for a specific result when a simple height-and-weight comparison would have narrowed the pool immediately.
