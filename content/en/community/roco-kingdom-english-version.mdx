A few field-tested habits worth adopting from day one:

- **Catch duplicates.** Individual size and weight variation means two Jinies of the same species are not interchangeable.
- **Rotate your team often.** The 18-class counter ecosystem punishes one-trick lineups, and PvP equalizes levels anyway.
- **Play with a friend when possible.** Shared EXP and gathering rewards make co-op strictly more efficient than solo play.
- **Read skill traits, not just damage numbers.** Community discussion around the beta suggests trait synergies decide more duels than raw power.
