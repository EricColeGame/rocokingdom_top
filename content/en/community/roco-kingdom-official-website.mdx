## How to Pre-Register on the Roco Kingdom Official Website

Pre-registration is free, takes about two minutes, and does not require you to own the game yet. The flow is straightforward, but there is one detail that trips people up: your account email is also your reward-claim email later, so pick an address you will still control at launch.

| Step | Action | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the [official Roco Kingdom website](https://rocokingdom.com/) | Bookmark it; test notices appear here first |
| 2 | Create or sign in to a Level Infinite account | Use a personal email, not a work or school address |
| 3 | Click the pre-register button on the homepage | The confirmation email arrives within minutes |
| 4 | Confirm your email | Unconfirmed sign-ups may not count toward rewards |
| 5 | Optionally invite friends for extra test chances | Invites only help while a test sign-up is open |

After you register, you receive a confirmation message noting that the same email will be used to sign in and claim in-game rewards after launch. Keep that message — it is effectively your receipt.

If your confirmation does not arrive, check spam and promotional folders first. Community reports suggest that Gmail and Outlook users occasionally see the message filtered, and re-requesting too many times can slow things down further.

### What About the Invite Link?

During an active test recruitment period, the site offers a shareable invite link. The rules are simple: complete the sign-up survey first, then invite friends. Inviting people before you have registered yourself does not generate credit. Players who bring in more successful pre-registrations reportedly receive higher priority during selection — but this is a boost, not a guarantee.

### Download Instructions by Platform

If you did secure a spot, the download process differs depending on where you play. The site lays out three paths, and mixing them up is the most common source of "I can't get in" complaints.

| Platform | How You Get the Build | Watch Out For |
|---|---|---|
| Android | Google Play link from the site, signed in with your Google account | Your Level Infinite account must be tied to a Gmail address |
| iOS | Apple invitation email, then TestFlight, then a redemption code | Do not forward the code; sharing it can void your access |
| PC | PC launcher from the site, then log in and hit Download | Use the exact account that received beta access |

The single most important rule: log in only with the Level Infinite account that was granted access. The site warns that using a different account may block you entirely, and that kind of block is not something support can fix quickly mid-test.

### Reading Community Reports Carefully

Community discussion around any closed beta tends to produce three things: genuine tips, survivable misinformation, and a lot of frustration about access. When you read player experience posts, check whether the poster was actually in the test. Someone who never got in cannot tell you how combat feels, no matter how confident the post sounds.

A reasonable filter: trust posts that describe specific mechanics or UI details, and treat anything about launch dates, reward eligibility, or region availability as unverified until the **Roco Kingdom official website** says the same thing.
