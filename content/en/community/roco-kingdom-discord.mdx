## Discord Etiquette: Rules, Roles, and Reputation

Every large gaming server develops an informal economy of attention. People who are helpful get answered faster; people who spam get muted. The Roco Kingdom Discord is no different, and a little etiquette goes a long way.

**Do:**

- Read pinned messages in a channel before asking a question
- Keep LFG posts specific — platform, region, time window, goal
- Use spoiler tags for story content and unreleased creature details
- Report scams and impersonators to moderators instead of starting a public argument
- Thank people who actually help — it costs nothing

**Don't:**

- Post the same LFG message in five channels at once
- Argue about tier lists in the help channel
- Share datamined content if the rules prohibit it
- DM strangers asking for account access or "free" beta keys

Role systems vary by server, but most gaming communities use a similar ladder:

| Role tier | How you get it | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| New member | Join and verify | Read-only or limited channels |
| Verified member | Accept rules / react to a prompt | Full chat access |
| Platform roles | Self-select (PC, Xbox, PlayStation) | Platform-specific LFG channels |
| Region roles | Self-select by time zone | Better matchmaking |
| Contributor / veteran | Sustained helpful activity or moderator grant | Extra perks, testing pings |
| Moderator | Appointed by staff | Enforcement tools |

Community reports suggest platform and region roles are the single most useful thing to set up early. It's the difference between "anyone want to group?" and a ping that reaches people who are actually online at the same time as you, on the same hardware.
