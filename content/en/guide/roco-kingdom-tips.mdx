## Build Your Foundation Before Launch

Roco Kingdom is developed by Morefun Studios and uses a Level Infinite account for sign-in and reward claiming. That single detail matters more than most players realize: your account email is the key that ties your pre-registration rewards, beta access, and eventual in-game goodies together. Use one email address consistently and keep it active.

Pre-registration is still the simplest free win available. Signing up on [the official Roco Kingdom website](https://rocokingdom.com/) unlocks the exclusive "Drowsiquack" Name Card when the open beta arrives, and it also puts you in the pool for test access through Lucky Drops. The closed beta phase — branded the Quack Quack Test — ran its main sign-up window through late October 2026, with the test itself scheduled to begin October 27, 2026 at 04:30 UTC. If you missed that window, pre-registration remains the path forward.

| Platform | What to do now | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| PC | Wishlist on Steam and prepare the PC launcher | Beta builds and launch clients have historically gone through the launcher first |
| Android | Pre-register via Google Play with the same email as your Level Infinite account | Test access is often granted to the Google Play account, not a separate login |
| iOS | Watch for TestFlight invitations from an Apple domain | Apple beta distribution requires TestFlight and a personal redemption code |
| PlayStation / Xbox | Wishlist the game on your console storefront | Wishlists help you catch launch-day availability and pre-load windows |

A quick word of caution from the official beta notices: test links and Apple redemption codes are personal. Forwarding them can lock you out of the test entirely. Treat them like a password.

## Know Your Jinies: Catching Tips That Pay Off Later

Jinies are the creatures you meet, catch, and journey with, and "twin with your Jini" is the emotional core of the whole experience. The official roster already showcases several named Jinies — Dimo, Meevy, Sparka, Cerulla, Vargyr, and Duckiji — with Dimo presented as a Light-type whose story is about courage and stubborn dreams. The rest of the roster is deliberately mysterious, which is exactly why your early catching habits matter.

Three catching habits worth building immediately:

- **Catch duplicates early, not late.** Duplicate Jinies give you flexibility for trading, breeding-style systems, or role experiments. Storage is cheaper than regret.
- **Log what you catch.** A simple note on type, location, and time of day turns a random collection into a searchable map of your world.
- **Don't over-invest in your first favorite.** Spread early upgrade materials across three or four Jinies until you understand the meta. Community reports from beta testers consistently suggest that early over-investment is the most common regret.

## Team Building: Strategy Over Stats

The official pitch is blunt about this: "Strategy over stats. Outsmart your rivals in total fair PvP." That single line should shape every team you build. In a game that promises fair PvP, raw power gaps are minimized, which means coverage, counters, and decision-making do the heavy lifting.

| Team role | Job on the team | Habit that makes it work |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor | Absorbs pressure and holds the field | Pick one Jini you trust in bad matchups and learn its limits |
| Sweeper | Converts small advantages into wins | Practice its combo lines in low-stakes fights before ranked play |
| Counter pick | Shuts down a specific popular strategy | Build two, not one — metas shift faster than single counters |
| Utility | Controls tempo with status, buffs, or disruption | Track which effects stack and which overwrite each other |
| Flex slot | Covers whatever your core is missing | Rotate this slot weekly based on what you keep losing to |

A practical rule: for every three Jinies you love, keep one you respect. The one you respect is the one that wins the matchup you keep dodging. Also resist the urge to build six attackers. Fair PvP formats reward teams that can answer a question, not teams that ask the same one six times.

## Open-World Exploration and Resource Habits

With 400+ Jinies roaming wild, the open world is the actual content engine — not a hallway between fights. Efficient explorers treat the map as a resource map.

| Priority | Time investment | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Unlock traversal and fast-travel points first | Low | Everything else gets faster for the rest of your playthrough |
| Sweep each region's edges before its center | Medium | Edge spawns are usually less contested and reveal hidden routes |
| Note weather and time-of-day spawn shifts | Low | Some Jinies only appear under specific conditions |
| Bank upgrade materials before you need them | Medium | Removes the mid-game grind wall almost entirely |
| Revisit cleared areas after major story beats | Low | New spawns and events frequently unlock behind progression |

Two more habits that separate comfortable players from broke ones: never spend your last stack of a rare material on a cosmetic or a sidegrade, and always keep one unopened reward cache in reserve for when a limited-time event demands a resource you've already burned.

## PvP Tips for Total Fair PvP

Because Roco Kingdom advertises fair PvP, you cannot buy your way to a win. You can, however, prepare your way to one.

| PvP habit | Why it wins games |
|---|---|
| Scout before you commit | Knowing the opposing lead changes your opening pick entirely |
| Play the first turn for information, not damage | One turn of scouting often decides the next ten |
| Track your losses, not just your wins | Patterns in defeats reveal the exact hole in your team |
| Set a stop rule | Three losses in a row means it's time to review, not requeue |
| Practice one team to mastery | Depth beats breadth in fair formats where stats are normalized |

Community reports from testers emphasize the same thing repeatedly: the players who climb aren't the ones with the rarest Jinies, they're the ones who understand two or three matchups deeply. If you want one more layer of Roco Kingdom tips to internalize, make it this — depth compounds, breadth dilutes.

## FAQ

**Do I need to pre-register to get Roco Kingdom tips like the Drowsiquack Name Card?**
Yes. The Name Card is a pre-registration reward claimed after launch, so sign up on the official site with the same Level Infinite email you plan to play with.

**How many Jinies are in Roco Kingdom?**
The official site advertises more than 400 unique Jinies in the wild at launch-scale content. The full roster beyond the featured six — Dimo, Meevy, Sparka, Cerulla, Vargyr, and Duckiji — is still being discovered.

**Is Roco Kingdom pay-to-win in PvP?**
The official messaging promises "total fair PvP" with strategy over stats. That's a design claim rather than a guarantee, so treat early community reports as player experience until the game is widely available.

**What's the single most useful piece of Roco Kingdom tips for a brand-new player?**
Slow down on upgrades and speed up on catching. A broad, well-documented collection plus a focused three-Jini core will carry you further than any single rare catch.
