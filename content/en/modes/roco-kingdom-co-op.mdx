## What Roco Kingdom Co-op Actually Includes

Roco Kingdom is an open-world creature collector from Morefun Studios where you play as a Roco, forge pacts with 400+ Jinies, attend classes at the Magic Academy, build a home, and explore the Continent of Carosia. Co-op isn't a separate mode bolted on afterward — the Steam feature list carries Online Co-op and Cross-Platform Multiplayer right beside Single-player and Online PvP.

The store page describes co-op in three concrete terms: riding the same Jini, holding hands to share rewards, and making creature catching easier when you're together.

| Co-op feature | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Co-riding a Jini | Two players travel on one mount and go wherever they want | Nobody gets left behind on long cross-map trips |
| Hand-holding | Links players so gathering rewards and battle EXP are shared | Both players progress even when only one gathers or fights |
| Assisted catching | Catching becomes easier and more fun with a partner | Fewer missed throws, less downtime between encounters |
| Cross-platform multiplayer | Play with friends on other supported platforms | Your group isn't split by hardware |
| In-game chat | Built-in communication channel | Coordination without third-party apps |

The framing on the official listing is deliberately low-pressure. Morefun Studios pitches co-op as "stress-free world exploration" — the point isn't competitive advantage, it's removing the friction of hiking across Carosia by yourself.

## Co-op vs. Solo: What Really Changes

Roco Kingdom supports both single-player and online co-op, and the game is fully playable alone. The difference is mostly about pacing and shared rewards rather than content gating.

| Activity | Playing solo | Playing Roco Kingdom co-op |
|---|---|---|
| Travel | You move at your own pace | Co-ride one Jini and stay in sync |
| Gathering | You keep 100% of what you collect | Hand-holding shares gathering rewards |
| Battles | You earn battle EXP alone | EXP is shared while linked |
| Catching | Standard catch flow | Catching is easier with a partner |
| Costumes | Match outfits to your Jini | Twin-style screenshots are more fun with a friend |
| PvP duels | Turn-based, level-equalized duels | Compare builds and 18-class matchups between sessions |

One important clarification: the store page lists Online PvP as a separate feature from Online Co-op. Duels are described as fair, turn-based battles with equalized Jini levels and an 18-class counter ecosystem, so co-op is best understood as a world-exploration and progression partnership rather than a team battle format.
