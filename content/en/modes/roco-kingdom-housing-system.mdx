If a home exists, the most likely design is an instanced plot tied to your account and reachable from the open world. That keeps the shared map clean while still letting you decorate freely. Look for a fast-travel point or a menu shortcut once the feature is live.

## Decoration Priorities: A Practical Spending Plan

If you have played any game with a decoration economy, you already know the trap: you blow your entire budget on a sofa and then discover you can't afford storage. The plan below keeps your home functional first and beautiful second.

- **Buy space before style.** Extra rooms, expanded plots, and storage slots pay off every single session.
- **Pick one visual theme and commit.** Mixing five aesthetics produces a cluttered look that costs more than it impresses.
- **Hold currency until the first event.** Limited-time furniture drops are usually better value than launch-day basics.
- **Craft duplicates deliberately.** If a recipe is cheap and the item is popular, extra copies are great trade or gift material.
- **Leave 20% of your budget unspent.** Every housing system in this genre ships a surprise unlock two weeks in.

| Area of Your Home | Share of Early Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Space and storage upgrades | 40% | Non-negotiable foundation |
| Functional furniture | 20% | Crafting benches, wardrobes, utility items |
| Creature display setup | 20% | The centerpiece for a collector |
| Pure decoration | 15% | Save the showpieces for later |
| Reserve fund | 5% | For surprise event items and unlocks |

Community reports suggest that players who front-load utility purchases regret it less than players who chase cosmetics first. That pattern holds across nearly every base-building game released in the last decade.

## Where Housing Fits in Progression and Social Play

Roco Kingdom has publicly committed to "strategy over stats" and total fair PvP. That commitment matters enormously for how the Roco Kingdom housing system should be designed. If a decorated home grants combat buffs, the fairness promise breaks. If it grants convenience — storage, travel, display — it enhances the game without tilting the arena.

The smartest assumption is that housing will be **cosmetic and organizational**, not competitive. That's good news. It means you can invest in your home without worrying that you're falling behind on the PvP ladder, and it means the feature stays optional for players who just want to catch Jinies and battle.

Socially, a home base is the natural home for the twin system. Pairing your Jini and then showing it off in a personalized space is a loop that writes itself. If Morefun Studios leans into that connection, housing becomes less of a side activity and more of a second progression track.

For players still waiting on access, the practical move is to pre-register through the [official Roco Kingdom website](https://rocokingdom.com/) so you're eligible for Lucky Drops and future test waves. That is also where any housing announcement will land first.

## FAQ

**Is the Roco Kingdom housing system confirmed?**

**When would a housing feature likely arrive?**
If it follows the pattern of similar open-world collectors, housing typically lands after launch once the core collection and combat loops are stable. The Quack Quack Test closed beta beginning October 27, 2026, is the first realistic place to see hints.

**Will housing affect PvP balance?**
It shouldn't. Roco Kingdom has publicly emphasized fair PvP where strategy beats stats, so the Roco Kingdom housing system is most likely to stay cosmetic and organizational. Any home perk that grants combat advantage would contradict that design promise.

**What should I focus on first if housing launches?**
Space, storage, and creature display. Those three make your collection easier to manage every day. Decoration can wait — the best-looking homes are built by players who solved the functional problems first.
