## Inside the Quack Quack Test: The Closed Beta That Precedes Launch

The Quack Quack Test is the final large-scale stress test before launch, and it is invite-only. Sign-ups ran from August 26 to October 20, 2026, with priority going to players who completed the survey and invited friends who pre-registered. If you missed that window, you are not locked out of everything — the site still accepts pre-registrations for a chance at test access through Lucky Drops.

| Milestone | Date / time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-up window | Aug. 26 – Oct. 20, 2026 | Closed |
| Invite-priority selection | Rolling during sign-up | More invites, higher priority |
| Beta start | Oct. 27, 2026, 04:30 UTC | Pre-download available beforehand |
| Beta platforms | iOS, Android, PC | No console beta announced |
| Beta languages | English, Japanese | Selected during the survey |
| Access requirement | Level Infinite account flagged for beta | Other accounts may be blocked |

Access logistics differ by platform, and this is where people usually trip up:

- **Android:** The invite link opens Google Play. Sign in with the Google account tied to your beta access and install the test build.
- **PC:** Download the launcher, log in with the Level Infinite account that received access, and hit Download.
- **iOS:** Apple sends an invitation email, you install TestFlight, then redeem the code from that email to install the build.

Apple's redemption codes are personal. Forwarding them to a friend can invalidate your own access — the beta terms are explicit that consequences fall on you. Community reports from earlier test waves also suggest that logging in with the wrong account can lock you out of the session entirely, so double-check before you queue.
