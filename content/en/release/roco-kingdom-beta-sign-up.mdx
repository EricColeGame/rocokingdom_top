## How the Roco Kingdom Beta Sign Up Process Worked, Step by Step

If you're preparing for a future round, or you want to confirm you completed every required step this time, here's the exact sequence the official flow used.

1. **Create or sign in to a Level Infinite account.** This is the identity that gets flagged for access. Beta invitations are tied to it, and logging in with a different account can block entry entirely.
2. **Pre-register first.** The site gates the test application behind pre-registration — clicking "Join the Test" before registering returns an error prompt.
3. **Complete the sign-up survey.** This step is mandatory. Skipping it disqualifies you regardless of how many friends you invite.
4. **Optionally invite friends.** Each friend who pre-registers through your personal invite link raises your priority in the selection pool.
5. **Watch your inbox.** Confirmation emails arrive immediately; selection results follow after recruitment closes.

The survey itself was more than a formality — it collected platform preference and hardware data used to distribute test slots.

| Survey section | What it asked | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Platforms | iOS, Android, PC (multi-select) | Determines which build you're eligible to receive |
| Account emails | Level Infinite email + Google Play email | Access is granted to the matching store account |
| Device specs | iPhone/iPad model, Android RAM, PC GPU tier | Used for compatibility filtering |
| Play history | Genres played in the last 6 months | Helps the team weight feedback cohorts |
| Discovery source | Where you heard about the game | Marketing attribution |

One detail that tripped people up: Android testers receive access through their Google Play account, so if your Level Infinite account wasn't registered with a Gmail address, the Play Store download may simply not appear for you. Community reports flagged this repeatedly during the registration window.

## Platform-by-Platform: How Access Is Delivered

The delivery method differs on every platform, and each has its own failure mode. Here's the breakdown.

| Platform | Delivery method | What you need ready | Common pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| Android | Google Play test build link | Google Play email matched to your Level Infinite account | Non-Gmail accounts may not see the download |
| iOS | TestFlight invite from an Apple domain | Redemption code from the invitation email | Forwarding the code can void your access |
| PC | PC launcher download | Level Infinite login on the granted account | Signing in with a different account blocks entry |

Apple's flow deserves extra caution. The invitation comes from an Apple domain, and you must install TestFlight before redeeming the code. The official site explicitly states that test links and redemption codes are personal — passing them to a third party risks losing your own slot, and the consequences fall on you.

## Practical Tips to Improve Your Odds Next Time

Nothing here guarantees a slot, but these habits consistently correlate with getting in — and they cost almost nothing.

- **Register early in the window.** Invite-based priority rewards accumulation over time, so a two-month head start beats a two-day sprint.

Player experience in the community suggests that invite-link volume mattered more than raw sign-up speed during this cycle, though the official rules only promise "higher priority" — not a guaranteed slot. Treat any claim of a 100% acceptance method as noise.
