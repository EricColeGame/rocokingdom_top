## Why the Roco Kingdom Closed Beta Matters Right Now

The Roco Kingdom closed beta is the first real chance for players to step into a creature-collecting open world that has been building hype for months. ## What the Roco Kingdom Closed Beta Actually Is

The Roco Kingdom closed beta is a limited, invitation-only build of the game from developer Morefun Studios, branded on the official site as the **Quack Quack Test**. It is not an open beta. You cannot simply download it and start playing — access is tied to the specific Level Infinite account that received the invitation, and logging in with a different account may block your access entirely.

The test runs across three platforms at once: iOS, Android, and PC. Supported languages during the beta are English and Japanese. Because progress, rewards, and account binding all flow through a Level Infinite account, the email you register with becomes your long-term identity for the game — the official site explicitly warns players to keep that address unchanged so they can claim in-game rewards after launch.

## How Access to the Roco Kingdom Closed Beta Was Distributed

Invitations were not handed out at random. The official event rules describe a two-part process designed to reward players who completed the survey and brought friends along.

| Step | What Players Had to Do | Why It Mattered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click "Join the Test" and complete the sign-up survey | Mandatory — no survey, no eligibility |
| 2 | Select platforms (iOS, Android, PC) and device specs | Used to sort testers by hardware |
| 3 | Click "Invite Friends" and share a personal link | More successful pre-registrations meant higher selection priority |

According to community reports, players who invited multiple friends reported better outcomes than solo applicants, which lines up with the official language about priority during selection. That said, invitation count was never a guarantee — it was a weighting factor, not a lock.

## Platform-by-Platform Download Guide

Once an invitation lands, the next hurdle is actually installing the build. Each platform has its own path, and each has a specific trap worth knowing before you try.

| Platform | How to Install | Watch Out For |
|---|---|---|
| Android | Open the pre-download link, sign in to Google Play, install the test build | If your Level Infinite account is not linked to a Gmail address, Google Play may not deliver the build |
| PC | Download the PC launcher, log in with your invited Level Infinite account, hit Download | A different account will not have access |
| iOS | Apple sends an invitation email from an Apple domain; install TestFlight, then redeem the code | The link and code are personal — forwarding them can disqualify you |

The iOS path is the one that trips people up most. TestFlight redemption codes are single-user by design, and the official notice states plainly that sharing them may prevent you from participating, with consequences falling on the account holder. Treat your code like a password.

## Practical Tips to Improve Your Odds

Even with the main window closed, there are concrete actions that still move the needle.

- **Pre-register on the official site and keep the confirmation email.** The site uses that address to sign you in and deliver launch rewards. Changing it later creates avoidable headaches.
- **Use the same email everywhere.** Android testers in particular are told that test access is granted to the Google Play account, and matching it to your Level Infinite account avoids delivery failures.
- **Check spam and promotions folders.** Selection notices arrive by email, and the official site also posts results, so a missed message is not always fatal — but it is a delay.
- **Report your real device specs if you fill out any future survey.** Test cohorts are often built around hardware diversity, and inflated specs can lead to a build that runs poorly for you.
- **Follow the official channels.** The site lists X, Discord, YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, Reddit, Twitch, and Instagram as update channels, and news about additional test waves appears there first.

For the authoritative source on sign-up status, rewards, and platform links, check [Roco Kingdom's official pre-registration page](https://rocokingdom.com/) rather than third-party aggregators, which often lag behind by days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Roco Kingdom closed beta still open for sign-ups?**
No. The main survey and invite window ran from Aug. 26 to Oct. 20, 2026, and has officially closed. Pre-registration remains open, and the site says players can still receive test access through Lucky Drops.

**When does the Roco Kingdom closed beta start?**
Servers open on October 27, 2026, at 04:30 UTC+0. Invited players can pre-download ahead of time using the Google Play link, the PC launcher, or TestFlight for iOS.

**Can I share my beta invite or TestFlight code with a friend?**
You should not. The official notice states that iOS test links and redemption codes are for personal use only, and forwarding them may prevent you from participating.

**What do I get for pre-registering if I never get into the test?**
Pre-registration is tied to the Drowsiquack Name Card, which is delivered when you download the game at Open Beta launch. Beta access and the name card are separate rewards, so missing the test does not cancel the cosmetic.
