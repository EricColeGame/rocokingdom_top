## How to Complete the Roco Kingdom Download on Each Platform

Each platform handles the Roco Kingdom download a little differently, and the login rules are stricter than most betas. Read the section that matches your device before you start clicking.

### PC: PC Launcher First, Steam Later

PC testers receive a launcher link rather than a Steam key. The flow is short but unforgiving:

1. Open the pre-download link from your beta confirmation notice.
2. Install the PC Launcher.
3. Sign in with the **exact** Level Infinite account that was granted access.
4. Hit Download inside the launcher to pull the test build.

The official notice is blunt about account mismatches: logging in with a different account may block your access entirely. If you share a PC, log out of any other Level Infinite session first. Steam and Epic wishlists are still worth joining, since that is where the eventual open beta and launch build will live.

### Android: Google Play, With One Catch

Android testers install through Google Play. Tap the pre-download link, sign in with the Google account tied to your test access, and install the test build like any other app.

The catch is account linkage. If your Level Infinite account was not registered with a Gmail address, the Google Play route may not recognize you. Community reports from earlier test waves suggest the cleanest setup is using the same email for both your Level Infinite account and your Google Play profile. If those two already diverge, sort it out before the test window opens rather than during it.

### iOS: TestFlight and a Redemption Code

Apple testers never see a store page. Instead, Apple sends an invitation email from an Apple domain. Follow it to install TestFlight, then redeem the code included in that email to pull the game.

Two warnings apply. First, the test link and redemption code are single-user — forwarding them to a friend usually means neither of you plays. Second, check your spam folder aggressively; Apple-domain invites are exactly the kind of mail that gets filtered.

## Roco Kingdom Download Troubleshooting: Common Hiccups

Most failed installs trace back to account identity, not bandwidth. Work through this table before you open a support ticket.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Store page says "unavailable" | Signed into the wrong account | Sign out and back in with the exact account that received access |
| Google Play shows no test build | Level Infinite account not linked to Gmail | Use the matching Google account; resolve mismatches with support |
| Launcher login rejected | A second account is active on the machine | Fully sign out of Level Infinite, then log in with the granted account |
| No Apple email arrived | Invite still rolling out or filtered | Check spam and promotions folders; rollout is gradual |
| Redemption code fails | Code already used or shared | Codes are personal; request help through official channels |
| Download stalls at 99% | Disk space or a background driver update | Free up storage, pause other downloads, retry |

One more habit worth building: never let a third party log into your account "to install it for you." Beta access is tied to a single identity, and a well-meaning friend can lock you out of the very test you were invited to.

## FAQ

**Is the Roco Kingdom download free?**
Yes. Pre-registration, beta access, and the eventual launch client are all free to obtain. There is no paid early-access tier and no legitimate paid shortcut into the closed beta — anyone selling "guaranteed access" is scamming you.

**Can I do a Roco Kingdom download right now without an invite?**
Not for the game client itself. Outside the Quack Quack Test, the only actions available today are pre-registering on the official site and wishlisting on Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation, or Xbox. Lucky Drops remain the fallback route for leftover test slots.

**Will my pre-registration rewards survive until launch?**
They should, as long as you keep the same email on your Level Infinite account. The Drowsiquack Name Card is tied to that address, so changing it mid-flight is the easiest way to lose a reward you already earned.

**Does the Roco Kingdom download work on Mac or consoles?**
No native Mac client has been announced. Console players can wishlist on PlayStation and Xbox, but those builds are not part of the current test — the live Roco Kingdom download paths are Windows, Android, and iOS only.
